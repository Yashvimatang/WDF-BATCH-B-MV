About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a prominent private university located in Changa, in the Anand district of Gujarat, India.

Established in 2009 under the Gujarat Private University Act, CHARUSAT is a UGC-approved university and is accredited with an "A+" grade by NAAC.

About the University

CHARUSAT provides quality education in various fields including engineering, technology, computer applications, management, pharmacy, health sciences, and other professional programs.

The university focuses on providing students with knowledge, practical skills, research opportunities, and professional development to prepare them for successful careers.

Information Technology

The Information Technology program focuses on developing students' knowledge of programming, software development, web technologies, databases, computer networks, and modern information technology concepts.

Students get opportunities to improve their technical skills through practical sessions, projects, assignments, and other academic activities.

About StudentHub

StudentHub is a centralized student academic portal designed to help students manage their academic activities in one place.

The portal provides easy access to important academic information and resources.

StudentHub Features

  • View subject-wise attendance records.
  • Check and manage academic assignments.
  • Access study notes and useful learning resources.
  • View examination results and academic performance.
  • Check daily and weekly class timetables.
  • Receive important college announcements and notifications.

Our Goal

The main goal of StudentHub is to provide students with a simple and organized platform where they can access important academic information quickly and efficiently.
